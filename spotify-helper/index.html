Link your Spotify to the LED disc

One quick login so the disc can show the album art of whatever you are playing.

  1. Tap the green button and log in to Spotify.
  2. Tap Agree to let the disc see what is playing. It can only read playback — it cannot control anything.
  3. You will come back here and get a code to copy.

Your Spotify password is only ever typed on Spotify's own page.

Getting your code…

All set. Copy this code:

  1. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi called LED-DISC-SETUP.
  2. The setup page opens by itself (if not, open 192.168.4.1).
  3. Choose your Wi-Fi, type its password and paste the code.

Keep this code private: anyone who has it can see what you are listening to. If it ever leaks, remove “LED Disc” under Spotify account → Apps and run this page again.
